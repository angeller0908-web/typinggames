# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 13, 2026_

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

- **Analytics** — if you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics to count page views and detect site issues. Google may set analytics cookies and process usage data according to its own privacy terms.
- **Local storage** — your personal-best scores are saved on your own device. We never see them.
- **Server logs** — Cloudflare Pages, our host, keeps brief access logs for abuse prevention.

## What we never collect

- We do not have user accounts.
- We do not collect names, emails, or phone numbers unless you contact us voluntarily.
- We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties.
- We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

## Cookies

A cookie consent banner appears on first visit. You can accept, reject, or change your choice anytime via the **Cookies** link in the footer. Rejecting blocks all non-essential cookies, including analytics and ads.

## Advertising

If we serve ads, the ad network may set cookies. The cookie banner will reflect this. We use only major ad networks (Google AdSense, Mediavine, Ezoic) that publish their own privacy policies. We do not allow advertisers to identify individual users.

## Your rights

If you are in the EU, UK, or California, you have the right to request what personal data we have about you, request deletion, and lodge complaints with your data-protection authority. Since we do not maintain user accounts and do not collect personal data, we typically have nothing to show you — but we are happy to confirm that in writing if you ask.
