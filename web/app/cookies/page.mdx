# Cookies Policy

_Last updated: May 13, 2026_

A cookie is a small file your browser stores so a site can remember things between visits. This page explains the cookies we use and your control over them.

## Strictly necessary

We use a single technical localStorage key (`tq_consent_v1`) to remember your cookie choice. This is not a cookie in the strict sense and does not leave your device. It is required for the consent banner to work.

## Analytics (only with consent)

If you accept cookies, we load Plausible Analytics. Plausible is a privacy-friendly tool that does not use cookies and does not collect personal information. We get aggregate page-view counts.

## Advertising (only with consent)

If we serve ads, the ad network may set cookies. You will see them appear in your browser's developer tools after consent is given. Rejecting consent blocks all advertising cookies.

## Personal-best scores

Game scores are saved on your device using localStorage under keys prefixed with `tq_best_`. These never leave your device. To clear them, use your browser's "clear site data" tool.

## Changing your choice

You can change your cookie choice anytime. Open your browser dev tools, type `localStorage.removeItem('tq_consent_v1')`, refresh the page, and the consent banner will appear again.
